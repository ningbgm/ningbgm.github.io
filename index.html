NingBGM: Multimodal Scene Understanding for Collaborative Video-to-Music Generation

[Abstract] [Comparisons] [Examples]

Anonymous authors

Abstract

Video-to-music generation requires coherent musical conditions derived from scene content, affect, and context. We present NingBGM, a framework that translates multimodal scene evidence into conditions for collaborative music generation. The framework integrates video with available audio, images, and text into time-ordered scene records. Profile-guided experts develop a shared musical blueprint from these records, with consistency checks guiding revision before synthesis. We evaluated NingBGM using seven automatic metrics and a 20-participant listening study. On CSD-200, NingBGM-BGM led four automatic metrics and all three applicable mean listening scores. On TB-Match, it led generated systems in all three semantic-similarity metrics and mean listening scores. These results support scene-guided planning for thematically suitable music, with performance varying across quality dimensions.
